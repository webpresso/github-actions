# Qualify caller setup resolution

## Product wedge anchor

- **Stage outcome:** The public reusable-workflow stage delivers deterministic, tokenless tool installation for Webpresso consumer CI.
- **Consuming surface:** .github/workflows/self-test.yml exercises .github/actions/setup-webpresso-toolchain/action.yml and .github/actions/setup-wp/action.yml on GitHub-hosted runners.
- **New user-visible capability:** Consumer workflows can rely on qualified Vite+ manifest/catalog resolution and a setup-wp cache miss that demonstrably becomes a cache hit without credentials.

## Summary

Qualify the consumer setup composite's range/catalog resolution against an explicit latest-fallback control, and setup-wp partial-poison-seed-to-fresh-runner-hit behavior, without changing public release routing or credentials.

#### Task 99.2: Qualify setup-wp poison-seed-to-fresh-hit behavior

**Status:** __wp-task-status__
**Wave:** 2
**Lane:** qa
**Depends:** Task 99.1
**Produces:** runner-setup-wp-cache-receipt
**Consumes:** runner-setup-vp-resolution-receipt, public-tokenless-setup-wp-contract

Seed a deterministic non-executable poisoned cache payload, require setup-wp's public miss path to replace its bytes, archive only cache subtree for one day, then restore in fresh job and prove invalid-network cache hit republishes and runs exact binary.

**Acceptance:**
- [_] Seed writes a unique poison marker into non-executable wp/0.0.6/x64/wp, invokes public local setup-wp, proves poison marker is absent, proves executable, and runs --help.

- [_] Fresh hit downloads with actions/download-artifact@d3f86a106a0bac45b974a628896c90dbdf5c8093, restores its own cache, invokes local setup-wp with nonexistent release-repo, and proves its own WP_INSTALL_DIR, command resolution, and --help.
- [_] Parsed test asserts poison marker/removal, artifact isolation, pins, retention, invalid-network control, exact path and execution.
- [_] bun test and actionlint pass.
